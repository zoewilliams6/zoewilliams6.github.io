Identity & Culture:

My color and typography choices are deeply tied to my personal style. The color palette is a combination of soft pastel shades and deeper contrasting shades that resonate with my personal aesthetic. My typography was selected mainly for clarity, with the font “Raleway” providing a clean and easy-to-read feel that seemed most fitting and practical for a website. As for the visual elements, the button color and style seem most authentic to me, although there wasn't much customization beyond the changing of the button when you hover over it. Despite the customization limitations I faced, I find that the colors I chose are practical yet also display a feeling of personality.

Accessibility Considerations:

Accessibility requirements strongly guided my design decisions for this assignment. Despite the fact that we were required to design with accessibility in mind, I ensured sufficient contrast between text and background, selected legible font sizes, and implemented clear focus indicators for the links and buttons. Balancing personal expression with accessibility was sometimes challenging. For certain colors, the shades I initially preferred had low contrast ratios and had to be adjusted or paired with contrasting text (black or white). This assignment encouraged me to think critically about how design choices can be both expressive of the designer's own personality while also being inclusive, and how small adjustments can significantly improve usability for all users.

Design Process:

Creating a cohesive design system highlighted how interconnected design elements are. It was surprising how adjusting one component, like spacing or color, influenced the overall perception of the page. Establishing a modular scale for typography and a consistent spacing system provided a sense of intentionality rather than random design choices thrown together onto a page. I plan to apply this system to my portfolio project by using these established rules for typography, spacing, and components, ensuring consistency across pages and creating a professional, polished result.

Future Applications:

This design system can be expanded or refined for different contexts by adding new color palettes, font styles, or visual components, depending on the project. This design system is helpful as it acts as a backbone or foundation for future projects. The elements can more easily be altered depending on the context. Overall, this system establishes a foundation for scalable, consistent, and inclusive design that can evolve while maintaining core principles of accessibility and personal identity.
